FocusHub

a tiny personal command center

FocusHub is a personal desktop app for Windows and macOS. It gathers tasks and calendar events in one place and answers one question: “What should I do right now?”

What it does

  • Recommends one task to do now and a short list of what's next, with a plain-English reason.
  • Understands quick notes like “finish economics essay tomorrow 45 min”.
  • Optionally reads your Google Calendar to find free time and avoid scheduling over meetings or classes.
  • Focus timer, morning plan, weekly reset, local backups.

Private by design

Everything stays on your own computer. FocusHub has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Connected services (like Google Calendar) talk directly to the app on your computer — never through anyone else.
