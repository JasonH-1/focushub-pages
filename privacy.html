Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026
Short version: FocusHub keeps your data on your own computer. It has no servers, collects nothing, and never sells, shares or uploads your information.

Who this covers

FocusHub is a personal desktop application. It is used by its developer and is not offered as a service to the public.

Data FocusHub stores

Tasks, projects, settings, focus-session history and cached calendar events are stored in a database file on your own computer. There are no FocusHub accounts or servers, and FocusHub does not collect analytics, telemetry or crash reports.

Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, FocusHub asks for permission to:

  • Read your calendars and events (calendar.readonly), so it can show your schedule, find free time and avoid planning tasks over meetings or classes.
  • Create events (calendar.events), only if you turn this option on, and only when you explicitly choose to add a planned focus block to your calendar.

How this data is handled:

  • Calendar data is requested directly by the app on your computer and cached only in its local database. It is never sent to any server owned by FocusHub or anyone else.
  • Google access tokens are stored in your operating system's secure credential storage (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain), not in plain text.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No human reads your calendar data.

FocusHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other optional connections

If you connect Notion, FocusHub reads the databases you choose in order to import tasks. It never edits or deletes anything in Notion. If you enable a local AI model, FocusHub talks only to a model running on your own computer.

Removing your data

  • Click Disconnect in FocusHub's Integrations page to delete stored tokens and cached calendar events.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling FocusHub and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised on the project page.
